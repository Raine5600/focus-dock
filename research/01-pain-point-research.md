## Executive Summary

The #1 monetizable pain point in the ADHD + Notion space is **plansturbation** — spending hours building beautiful systems that get abandoned within 3 weeks. People don't need another $19 template with 14 databases. They need a **guided recovery protocol** that installs a minimal system in under an hour and teaches them *why* each piece exists for an ADHD brain.

This is hard for creators to DIY because it requires:
- ADHD-specific psychology (dopamine traps, choice overload, shame avoidance)
- Precise Notion formulas/rollups that work as a unified system
- Task-sequence logic (executive dysfunction / task initiation)
- Maintenance design that doesn't punish missed days

**What people say:**
- "I spent 4 hours customizing colors and relations. Felt amazing. Haven't opened it since Wednesday."
- "I've downloaded 5 ADHD Notion templates this year. All abandoned."
- "The template looked gorgeous in the screenshot. Using it felt like homework."

**Why they'll pay $27:** They've already spent $19–$99 on templates that failed. A *recovery guide* that prevents repeat failure is cheaper than the next template impulse buy.

**Why creators can't easily DIY:** Requires understanding dopamine economy of system-building vs. actual work, plus a counter-intuitive "delete 80% of your setup" protocol.

### 2. Choice Overload / Decision Density (HIGH)
**Sources:** Iyengar-Lepper choice overload research (cited in forget.work), Gridfiti template roundups, Reddit frustration threads

**What people say:**
- "Should this go in Projects or Areas? Journal or Tasks? I gave up."
- "Every time I open Notion I make 12 micro-decisions before doing anything."
- "The Eisenhower matrix + habit tracker + mood log = overwhelm factory."

**Why they'll pay:** They want someone to make the decisions FOR them once, with a "good enough" default.

**Why creators can't DIY:** Most Notion creators sell *more* features. The anti-feature protocol (3 databases max) contradicts their own products.

---

### 3. Task Initiation / Executive Dysfunction (HIGH)
**Sources:** dramsch.net task sequences article, goblin.tools Magic ToDo, ADHD coach content

**What people say:**
- "Washing clothes isn't one task — it's 8 steps I can't see."
- "I put 8 tasks on my list, 7 are irrelevant, so I stop looking entirely."
- "I know what to do. I can't start."

**Why they'll pay:** Task-sequence logic with exact Notion formulas is technical and time-consuming to figure out.

**Why creators can't DIY:** Requires relations, rollups, and hide formulas — 2+ hours of trial-and-error even for power users.

---

### 4. Visible State / Streak Shame (MEDIUM-HIGH)
**Sources:** ADHD coach warnings, template reviews, abandonment patterns

**What people say:**
- "I missed one day on my habit tracker and now the whole dashboard is a monument to failure."
- "Broken streaks make me close the tab."
- "Sunday weekly review feels like facing a crime scene."

**Why they'll pay:** They need a system designed for *missed days* — no streaks, no guilt dashboards.

### 5. Maintenance Burden (MEDIUM)
**Sources:** Zapier ADHD app reviews, Akiflow "shiny object" pattern

**What people say:**
- "I have to motivate myself to open the system I built to motivate myself."
- "Weekly reviews take longer than the work."
- "Sync issues and broken relations killed my motivation."

**Why they'll pay:** An 11-minute Sunday reset beats a 45-minute weekly review ritual.
